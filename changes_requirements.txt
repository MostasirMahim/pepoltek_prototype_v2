A. Rate Parameters (Hardcoded in Engine)
Calculations apply published hourly add-ons, sourcing rates, and telemetry brackets:
● Offshore (South Asia / APAC Delivery Hub): Junior ($12/hr | $1,920/mo), Mid
($25/hr | $4,000/mo), Senior ($45/hr | $7,200/mo), Lead/Architect ($65/hr |
$10,400/mo).
● Nearshore (LATAM / CEE): Junior ($20/hr | $3,200/mo), Mid ($35/hr | $5,600/mo),
Senior ($60/hr | $9,600/mo), Lead/Architect ($85/hr | $13,600/mo).
● Onshore (US / UK / GCC): Mid ($55/hr | $8,800/mo), Senior ($95/hr | $15,200/mo),
Lead/Architect ($110/hr | $17,600/mo).
● Dedicated Virtual Recruiter / Sourcer Add-On: $8/hr to $20/hr ($1,280/mo to
$3,200/mo per dedicated sourcer).
B. Mathematical Calculation Logic
1. Traditional Agency Cost = Sum(Selected Roles Annual Salary * 0.20 Placement Fee)
Example: 1 Sr Engineer ($120,000) + 1 Mid Engineer ($80,000) = $200,000 * 20% =
$40,000 One-Time Fee.
2. Pepoltek Dedicated RPO Cost = Sum(Role Headcount * Regional Monthly Rate) +
Dedicated Recruiter Fee
Example: 1 Sr APAC Engineer ($7,200) + 1 Mid APAC Engineer ($4,000) + 1 Dedicated
Sourcer ($1,280) = $12,480/Month.
3. Calculated Net Client Savings = Traditional Agency Cost - (Pepoltek RPO Cost * First
Month Deployment)
Display Output: "Saves $27,520 in upfront placement fees while cutting time-to-fill from 45
days to 7 days."
